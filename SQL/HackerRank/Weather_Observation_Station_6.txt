# Question 11: Weather Observation Station 6

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Query the list of CITY names starting with vowels (i.e., a, e, i, o, or u) from STATION.
 Your result cannot contain duplicates.

### Solution

SELECT CITY
FROM STATION
WHERE LEFT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u');

### Explanation

- **Purpose**: Retrieves the names of cities from the `STATION` table that start with a vowel (A, E, I, O, or U).

### **Query Breakdown**
- **Selected Column**:
  - `CITY`: Selects the name of the city from the `STATION` table.

- **Data Source**:
  - `FROM STATION`: Pulls data from the `STATION` table.

- **Filtering**:
  - `WHERE LEFT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`:
    - `LEFT(CITY, 1)`: Extracts the first character from the `CITY` name.
    - `IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`: Checks if the first character of the city name is one of the lowercase vowels — 'a', 'e', 'i', 'o', or 'u'.

> **Note**: If the city names in the table have uppercase letters, this query might not work as intended. To make it case-insensitive, you could use `LOWER(LEFT(CITY, 1)) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`.

### **Result**:
- The output will be a list of city names from the `STATION` table where the first letter of the city's name is a vowel (A, E, I, O, or U).
