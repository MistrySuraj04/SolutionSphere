# Question 15: Weather Observation Station 10

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Query the list of CITY names from STATION that do not end with vowels. Your result cannot contain duplicates.

### Solution

SELECT DISTINCT CITY
FROM STATION
WHERE RIGHT(CITY, 1) NOT IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u');

### Explanation

### **Purpose**:
The query retrieves a distinct list of city names from the `STATION` table where the last letter of the city name is **not** a vowel (A, E, I, O, or U).

### **Query Breakdown**:
1. **Selected Column**:
   - `DISTINCT CITY`: Selects unique city names from the `STATION` table, ensuring no duplicates in the result.

2. **Data Source**:
   - `FROM STATION`: Specifies the `STATION` table as the source for the data.

3. **Filtering Condition**:
   - `WHERE RIGHT(CITY, 1) NOT IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`:
     - `RIGHT(CITY, 1)`: Extracts the last character of the city name.
     - `NOT IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`: Filters out cities where the last letter is one of the vowels ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', or 'u'). Only cities whose last letter is not a vowel will be included in the result.

> **Note**: If city names in the `STATION` table are stored with uppercase letters, this query might not work as expected. To ensure case-insensitivity, you can use the `LOWER()` function to convert the last letter to lowercase:
   - `LOWER(RIGHT(CITY, 1)) NOT IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`

### **Result**:
The result will be a list of distinct city names where the last letter of the city's name is **not** a vowel (A, E, I, O, or U).
