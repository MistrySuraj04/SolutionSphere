# Question 20: Employee Salaries

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Write a query that prints a list of employee names (i.e.: the name attribute) for employees in Employee having a salary greater than  per month who have been employees for less than  months. Sort your result by ascending employee_id.

### Solution

SELECT NAME
FROM EMPLOYEE
WHERE SALARY > 2000 AND MONTHS < 10
ORDER BY EMPLOYEE_ID ASC;

### Explanation

### **Purpose**:
The query retrieves the names of employees from the `EMPLOYEE` table whose salary is greater than 2000 and have worked for less than 10 months, then sorts them by employee ID in ascending order.

### **Query Breakdown**:
1. **Selected Column**:
   - `NAME`: Selects the name of the employee.

2. **Data Source**:
   - `FROM EMPLOYEE`: Specifies the `EMPLOYEE` table as the source of the data.

3. **Filtering Conditions**:
   - `WHERE SALARY > 2000`: Filters the results to include only employees whose salary is greater than 2000.
   - `AND MONTHS < 10`: Further filters the results to include only employees who have worked for less than 10 months.

4. **Sorting**:
   - `ORDER BY EMPLOYEE_ID ASC`: Sorts the results by the `EMPLOYEE_ID` in ascending order.

### **Result**:
The result will be a list of employee names whose salary is greater than 2000 and who have worked for less than 10 months, ordered by their employee ID in ascending order.
