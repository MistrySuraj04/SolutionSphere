
# Question 13: Weather Observation Station 8

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Query the list of CITY names from STATION which have vowels (i.e., a, e, i, o, and u) as both their first and last characters.
Your result cannot contain duplicates.

### Solution

SELECT DISTINCT CITY
FROM STATION
WHERE LEFT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')
AND RIGHT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u');

### Explanation

### **Purpose**:
The query retrieves a distinct list of city names from the `STATION` table where both the first and last characters of the city name are vowels (A, E, I, O, or U).

### **Query Breakdown**:
1. **Selected Column**:
   - `DISTINCT CITY`: Selects unique city names, ensuring no duplicates in the result.

2. **Data Source**:
   - `FROM STATION`: Specifies the `STATION` table as the source for the data.

3. **Filtering Conditions**:
   - `WHERE LEFT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`:
     - `LEFT(CITY, 1)`: Extracts the first character of the city name.
     - `IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`: Ensures that the first letter of the city name is one of the vowels ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', or 'u').
   - `AND RIGHT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`:
     - `RIGHT(CITY, 1)`: Extracts the last character of the city name.
     - `IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`: Ensures that the last letter of the city name is also one of the vowels.

> **Note**: If city names in the `STATION` table contain uppercase letters, this query might not work as intended. To make the query case-insensitive, you can apply the `LOWER()` function to both `LEFT()` and `RIGHT()` like this:
   - `LOWER(LEFT(CITY, 1)) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`
   - `LOWER(RIGHT(CITY, 1)) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`

### **Result**:
The result will be a list of unique cities where both the first and last characters of the city names are vowels.
