# Question 31: Weather Observation Station 13

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Query the sum of Northern Latitudes (LAT_N) from STATION having values greater than  and less than .
Truncate your answer to  decimal places.

### Solution

SELECT ROUND(SUM(LAT_N), 4)
FROM STATION
WHERE LAT_N > 38.7880 AND LAT_N < 137.2345;

### Explanation

### **Purpose**:
The query calculates the **sum of the `LAT_N` values** (latitude) in the **STATION** table for records where the `LAT_N` is within the specified range (greater than 38.7880 and less than 137.2345), and **rounds** the result to **4 decimal places**.

---

### **Query Breakdown**:

1. **Columns to Retrieve**:
   ```sql
   SELECT ROUND(SUM(LAT_N), 4)
   ```

   - **`SUM(LAT_N)`**:
     - Sums up the **`LAT_N`** values (latitude) from the **STATION** table for the rows that satisfy the filter condition.
     - This calculates the **total latitude** of the stations within the specified range.

   - **`ROUND(..., 4)`**:
     - Rounds the resulting sum of latitudes to **4 decimal places** for precision.

---

2. **Data Source**:
   ```sql
   FROM STATION
   ```

   - Specifies the **STATION** table as the source for the data.

---

3. **Filter Condition**:
   ```sql
   WHERE LAT_N > 38.7880 AND LAT_N < 137.2345
   ```

   - Filters the **LAT_N** values to include only those that are **greater than 38.7880** and **less than 137.2345**.
   - This ensures that only latitudes within a specific range are considered for the sum.

---

### **Example Walkthrough**

**STATION Table (example data):**

| **LAT_N** | **LONG_W** |
|-----------|------------|
| 39.1234   | 122.5678   |
| 40.5678   | 123.9876   |
| 38.0000   | 121.2345   |

---

### **Query Execution**:
1. **Filter the rows**:
   - The condition `LAT_N > 38.7880 AND LAT_N < 137.2345` will select the rows with the following `LAT_N` values:
     - 39.1234, 40.5678, 38.0000, and 136.9876

2. **Sum the selected `LAT_N` values**:
   - **39.1234 + 40.5678 + 38.0000 + 136.9876** = **254.6788**

3. **Round the result to 4 decimal places**:
   - `ROUND(254.6788, 4)` will return **254.6788** (since it is already at 4 decimal places).

---

### **Result**:
The result will be the rounded sum of `LAT_N` values for the stations within the specified latitude range:

| **ROUND(SUM(LAT_N), 4)** |
|-------------------------|
| 254.6788                |

---

This query is useful when you need to calculate the **total latitude** of locations (stations, in this case) within a specific **latitude range**, with the precision of **4 decimal places**.
