# Question 24: Revising Aggregations - Averages

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Query the average population of all cities in CITY where District is California.

### Solution

SELECT AVG(POPULATION)
FROM CITY
WHERE UPPER(DISTRICT) = 'CALIFORNIA';

### Explanation

### **Purpose**:
The query calculates the average population of cities in the `CITY` table that are located in the district of California.

### **Query Breakdown**:
1. **Selected Column**:
   - `AVG(POPULATION)`: Calculates the average of the `POPULATION` column for the cities that meet the filtering condition. This gives the average population of cities in California.

2. **Data Source**:
   - `FROM CITY`: Specifies the `CITY` table as the source of the data.

3. **Filtering Condition**:
   - `WHERE UPPER(DISTRICT) = 'CALIFORNIA'`: Filters the rows where the `DISTRICT` column (converted to uppercase using `UPPER()`) matches `'CALIFORNIA'`. This ensures that the comparison is case-insensitive.

### **Result**:
The result will be a single value representing the average population of cities located in the district of California in the `CITY` table.
