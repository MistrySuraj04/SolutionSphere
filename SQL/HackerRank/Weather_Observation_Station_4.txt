# Question 9: Weather Observation Station 4

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Find the difference between the total number of CITY entries in the table and the number of distinct CITY entries in the table.

### Solution

SELECT (COUNT(CITY) - COUNT(DISTINCT CITY))
FROM STATION;

### Explanation

- **Purpose**: Calculates the number of duplicate city names in the `STATION` table.
- **Explanation of the Calculation**:
  - `COUNT(CITY)`: Counts the total number of city entries in the `CITY` column, including duplicates.
  - `COUNT(DISTINCT CITY)`: Counts the number of unique city names in the `CITY` column, ignoring duplicates.
  - `(COUNT(CITY) - COUNT(DISTINCT CITY))`: Subtracts the count of distinct city names from the total count of city entries. This difference represents the total number of duplicate city names in the `STATION` table.
- **Data Source**:
  - `FROM STATION`: Pulls data from the `STATION` table.
- **Result**:
  - The result will be a single value that indicates how many city names are repeated in the `CITY` column.
