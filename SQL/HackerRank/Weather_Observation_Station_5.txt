# Question 10: Weather Observation Station 5

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Query the two cities in STATION with the shortest and longest CITY names,
as well as their respective lengths (i.e.: number of characters in the name).
If there is more than one smallest or largest city,
choose the one that comes first when ordered alphabetically.

### Solution

( SELECT CITY, LENGTH(CITY)
FROM STATION
GROUP BY CITY
ORDER BY LENGTH(CITY) ASC, CITY ASC
LIMIT 1 )

UNION ALL

( SELECT CITY, LENGTH(CITY)
FROM STATION
GROUP BY CITY
ORDER BY LENGTH(CITY) DESC, CITY DESC
LIMIT 1 )

### Explanation

- **Purpose**: Retrieves the city with the shortest name and the city with the longest name from the `STATION` table.

### **Query Breakdown**
1. **Subquery 1**:
   - **Objective**: Finds the city with the shortest name in terms of character length.
   - **Selected Columns**:
     - `CITY`: The name of the city.
     - `LENGTH(CITY)`: The length of the city's name in characters.
   - **Data Source**:
     - `FROM STATION`: Pulls data from the `STATION` table.
   - **Grouping**:
     - `GROUP BY CITY`: Ensures unique city names are processed to avoid duplicates.
   - **Sorting**:
     - `ORDER BY LENGTH(CITY) ASC, CITY ASC`: Sorts the city names by their length in ascending order, and for cities with the same length, sorts them alphabetically in ascending order.
   - **Limiting**:
     - `LIMIT 1`: Retrieves only the first city with the shortest name from the sorted list.

2. **UNION ALL**: Combines the results of **Subquery 1** and **Subquery 2** into a single result set, without eliminating duplicates.

3. **Subquery 2**:
   - **Objective**: Finds the city with the longest name in terms of character length.
   - **Selected Columns**:
     - `CITY`: The name of the city.
     - `LENGTH(CITY)`: The length of the city's name in characters.
   - **Data Source**:
     - `FROM STATION`: Pulls data from the `STATION` table.
   - **Grouping**:
     - `GROUP BY CITY`: Ensures unique city names are processed to avoid duplicates.
   - **Sorting**:
     - `ORDER BY LENGTH(CITY) DESC, CITY DESC`: Sorts the city names by their length in descending order, and for cities with the same length, sorts them alphabetically in descending order.
   - **Limiting**:
     - `LIMIT 1`: Retrieves only the first city with the longest name from the sorted list.

### **Result**:
- The output will have **two rows**:
  1. The city with the shortest name and its length.
  2. The city with the longest name and its length.
