
# Question 12: Weather Observation Station 7

**Platform:** HackerRank

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Problem Statement**

Query the list of CITY names ending with vowels (a, e, i, o, u) from STATION.
Your result cannot contain duplicates.

### Solution

SELECT DISTINCT CITY
FROM STATION
WHERE RIGHT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u');

### Explanation

- **Purpose**: Retrieves a distinct list of city names from the `STATION` table where the last letter of the city name is a vowel (A, E, I, O, or U).

### **Query Breakdown**
- **Selected Column**:
  - `DISTINCT CITY`: Selects unique city names from the `STATION` table, ensuring no duplicate city names are displayed.

- **Data Source**:
  - `FROM STATION`: Pulls data from the `STATION` table.

- **Filtering**:
  - `WHERE RIGHT(CITY, 1) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`:
    - `RIGHT(CITY, 1)`: Extracts the last character of the city name.
    - `IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`: Checks if the last character of the city name is one of the lowercase vowels — 'a', 'e', 'i', 'o', or 'u'.

> **Note**: If the city names in the table have uppercase letters, this query might not work as intended. To make it case-insensitive, you could use `LOWER(RIGHT(CITY, 1)) IN ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u')`.

### **Result**:
- The output will be a list of distinct city names from the `STATION` table where the last letter of the city's name is a vowel (A, E, I, O, or U).
